Review-first by design.

VATSorted does not pretend every file is correct. It organises the paperwork, keeps uncertain files visible, and prepares a cleaner pack for human review.

Uncertain files stay visible

Files with missing dates, unclear document types, or uncertain quarters are separated for review.

Cleaner accountant handover

Documents are grouped before review, instead of being sent as a messy folder or mixed inbox download.

Preparation, not tax advice

VATSorted prepares the document pack. Your accountant or bookkeeper still reviews VAT treatment and submits the return.

Missed or poorly organised VAT documents can mean extra admin, delayed reviews, or unclaimed input VAT. VATSorted is designed to make review easier, not to skip it.

Local-first by design.

VATSorted runs on your computer and creates review folders locally. Email access is optional and used only to find VAT-related attachments.

Runs locally

Runs on your own computer through a local browser-based interface.

  • Your working folders stay on your machine.
  • Not a cloud accounting portal.

Email scanning is optional

Gmail and Outlook are used only to find and download VAT-related attachments.

  • Manual upload remains available if you prefer not to connect email.

Preparation, not submission

Organises documents into folders and exports a review pack.

  • Does not submit VAT returns to HMRC.
  • Does not replace accountant or bookkeeper review.
