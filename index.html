Built for the messy part before the VAT return.

VATSorted helps prepare and organise VAT documents. It does not replace your accountant, give tax advice, or submit VAT returns.

Manual uploads

Upload PDF files and ZIP folders when invoices are already saved on your computer.

Email attachment scans

Scan Gmail and Outlook accounts for VAT-related attachments without manually searching old inboxes.

VAT quarter sorting

Group documents into VAT quarters, including a separate area for unknown dates or uncertain quarters.

Review categories

Sort files into Valid invoices, Needs review, Statements, Pro forma, and Unknown date/quarter.

Accountant ZIP export

Create a structured ZIP file that can be sent to an accountant or kept with VAT records.

Local-first app

VATSorted runs locally through a browser-based Streamlit interface on your own computer.
