Local-first by design.

VATSorted runs on your computer and creates review folders locally. Email access is optional and used for finding VAT-related attachments.

Runs locally

Runs on your own computer through a local browser-based interface.

  • Your working folders stay on your machine.
  • Not a cloud accounting portal.

Email scanning is optional

Gmail and Outlook are used to find and download VAT-related attachments.

  • Manual upload remains available if you prefer not to connect email.

Preparation, not submission

Organises documents into folders and exports a review pack.

  • Does not submit VAT returns to HMRC.
  • Does not replace accountant or bookkeeper review.
